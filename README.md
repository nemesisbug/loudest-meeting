## The line

> I bought noise-cancelling headphones to escape the office noise, and now I can hear my own thoughts, which turn out to be the loudest meeting of the day.

153 characters, one sentence. The everyday truth: open-plan offices are loud,
so we buy noise-cancelling headphones. The surprise at the end: once the
office is silent, the noise left is your own thoughts — and they hold the
loudest meeting of the day. Nothing in the line explains the joke; the twist
does the work.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt
```

No dependencies, no network. `main.ts` runs on Node 22 and writes the line
above to `/output/entry.txt`.
